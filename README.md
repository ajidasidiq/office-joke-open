One original one-line joke about office life, written for make.fun's "Make the AI Judge Laugh: the Office Joke Open" bounty. The program writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`, where the judge reads it and compares it two at a time against the other entries. The line takes the most tired piece of management advice — "think outside the box" — literally, and the punchline lands on the one perk every office worker actually wants: a window.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
